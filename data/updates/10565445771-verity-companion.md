## Update 10.09  — SHIPPED EARLY AS v689 ON 2026-09-08

1) **Messages refill mechanics**
   1. Daily rewards — classic D7 UI with messages as rewards. Unlock all — 399R
   2. Returning player rewards — when player was away for more than 6 days, a
      special icon appears on the screen + UI automatically opens when he enters.
      Player receives a one-time reward — 50msg, then a 3 days additional to D7
      track. D1 — 20msg, D2 — 25msg, D3 — 50msg

2) **Endings adjustment** — remove restriction to repeatedly have the same ending

3) **Like reward** — reward for liking the game and joining the group.
   Gives 50 messages and mischievous verity skin

4) **Playtime rewards rework** — add timer and Ready alert

5) **New personalities**
   1. Add Dreamity (former Poetic Verity) — add to the Personalities selector, price 99R
   2. Add Cruelty (red) — add to the Personalities selector, price 99R

## Update 04.09  — v684

1) **Alternative endings** — Best friends ending **DELAYED**. Owner will integrate
   something like a politeness system, so AI can evaluate player's messages. If
   politeness level is high enough after getting mad at the final scene, Verity
   calms down… then leads player to one of the buildings and locks all doors
   saying "See? You don't need anyone else". Final screen: "You're trapped here forever"

2) **Verity skins/personalities** — a shop UI where you can buy different Verity
   skins. Each skin changes the personality (messaging style), Verity's appearance
   and message box colour. First skin is free — player can get it for spending over
   50 messages. Personalities: Poetic Verity, Mischievous Verity

3) **Bugs and QoL**

## Update 28.08  — v678/v679

1) **Mystery Library Event** — spawns every hour for 10 minutes, countdown icon at
   the bottom of the screen. "The Library has appeared! Answer a question to earn
   free messages." Locked entrance with a random general knowledge question, 3
   attempts. Inside, players open a chest and receive 10–20 free messages.

2) **Alternative endings** — after 5 in-game days, Verity goes mad and kills the
   player. Two alternative endings. 2.1 More creepy ending: at the final scene add
   a dialogue option "Shut Verity down" → "Turn off 5 power generators before
   Verity gets you", 5 generators appear across the map; Verity follows the player,
   map darkens, FOV narrows; at the last generator Verity kills the player →
   ending screen.
