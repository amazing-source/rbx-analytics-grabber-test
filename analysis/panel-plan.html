Four constraints that reshape the brief

All four are measured, not assumed. Each removes or downgrades something the plan asks for, and it is cheaper to know now than after a week of modelling.

Read these before Phase 1

  • 01
    The RFY signals only start 2026-05-01 — on every game, identically. That is Roblox's own retention boundary, not our grab. The June discovery change sits just 45 days after it. So the pre/post-June test you asked for has, on the RFY side, 45 pre-days against 80 post-days on 9 games and none on 3. pre/post-June on RFY: weak on DAU/revenue: fine
  • 02
    Technical history is missing for most of every game's life. Between 8 and 551 days of each game's behavioural record has no crash, performance or error data behind it — it aged out before we ever grabbed. Any "did something break?" question before roughly July 2026 is unanswerable, and must be reported as absence of coverage, never as health.
  • 03
    The publish log is truncated at 100 versions on 10 of 12 games (F12). Don't Wake The Police's newest recorded publish is 2026-02-14 on a game that is live now — that is the cap, not the truth. Update timing is a control variable in Phases 1, 6 and 7, so this contaminates them until repaired from feedback.csv asset versions or an ErrorCount-by-PlaceVersion pull.
  • 04
    n = 12 games, and roughly one pullback each. Phase 7 asks for a predictive model; with about a dozen events, leave-one-game-out validation is close to anecdote and any feature importance will be unstable. Worth running as hypothesis generation, but I will not present a model as evidence, and pre/post-June splitting of an already-small event set is not defensible. Phase 7: hypothesis generation only

I

The plan

Ordered as briefed, with a feasibility call on each from the audit above. Descriptive work finishes before anything is modelled.

0

Repair update timing

do firstblocks 1, 6, 7

Not in the original brief, but it precedes Phase 1. Rebuild the publish log for the 10 capped games from feedback.csv asset versions and ErrorCount by PlaceVersion. Version first-seen dates are left-censored at the window edge — a version whose first point falls on the window's own start shipped earlier and was clipped, so it reads "on or before", never "on".

1

Reconstruct each lifecycle

fully supported

Objective pullback rule fixed before looking at any other metric: J0 is the first day at or after the all-time Home-reach peak where the 3-day mean falls below a threshold of that peak and stays below for 3 consecutive days. Sensitivity-tested at 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.80 and at 3 / 5 / 7 hold days; a result that only survives one setting is reported as such.

Reach is UniqueUsersWithImpressions filtered to HomeRecommendation — not the all-source total, which mixes in Friends and Search and blunts the very signal being dated.

2

Leading indicators of pullback

supportedretention cannot lead

Event-study on the J−28…J+28 windows as briefed. Two disciplines carried over from the work that has already failed on this question: every direction test gives both arrows the same lag search and a multiplicity correction — an asymmetric search manufactured five false "signal leads reach" results on Waterslide last week — and every candidate break reports its smear fraction, the share of candidate dates that also fire, because a trending series accepts a step almost anywhere.

Per the maturation table, D7 and D30 are structurally incapable of leading and enter as outcomes only.

3

Explore → expand → degradation

supported

The discriminating test, and the one already run on two games: partial correlation of each signal against log Home reach, controlling for a linear trend and weekday. The trend control is not optional — without it, reach quintiles on a young game are almost perfectly collinear with time-since-launch (r = +0.81 on Seal), and novelty decay reads as audience dilution.

Dilution is PTR falling while bounce, playtime and play-days hold. Failure at scale is PTR falling with bounce rising and play-days falling. Those get different reports.

4

Acquisition-source quality

fully supported, all 12

Conversion, D7, 7-day playtime and payer quality per source, weighted by UniqueUsersWithPlaySessions — the denominator. Rates alone mislead: on Seal, Friends converts at 40.5% against Home's 19.5%, which reads as the better channel until you see 343,887 users against 2,084,897.

Kept strictly separate from Phase 2: aggregate acquisition quality is not an RFY ranking signal, and Roblox states the ranking runs on RFY-acquired behaviour specifically.

5

Funnels

4 games2 thin2 unusable

Reconstruct the join → first action → first reward → progression → second-loop sequence where the steps exist, split by device, platform, acquisition source and place version.

A step's level is not a finding. A step sitting at 40% completion means nothing without a baseline it moved from — and on most of these games there is no baseline to move from. Funnels localise a churn detected elsewhere; they do not lead the diagnosis. Where a step does collapse, the chain is metric → feedback text → QA repro → Studio code, and the output is "phone players fail tutorial step 4 after build X", not "bounce got worse".

6

Common shock vs independent failure

supported on reachweak on RFY

Both alignments — calendar date, and days relative to each game's own peak — on weekday-and-trend residualised log reach. If several games lose residual reach on the same calendar dates while their own signals hold, platform or seasonality gains credibility. If declines line up better against each game's own lifecycle and are each preceded by that game's own deterioration, independent causes win.

The pre/post-June arm runs on reach and behaviour, not on RFY, for the reason in constraint 01.

7

Prediction

hypothesis generation only

Leave-one-game-out, time-ordered, features frozen at J−1, logistic regression before anything with more capacity, compared against a "days since peak" baseline that will probably be hard to beat. Reported with false positives and negatives by name, and sensitivity to the pullback definition.

With ~12 events this cannot establish that we have recovered anything about Roblox's ranking. If the model beats the baseline it is a hypothesis worth a prospective test on the next pullback, and that is the strongest claim available.

8

Intervention mapping

supported

Each recurring pattern gets an action, the metric that should move if we are right, the maturation-aware wait before judging it, and the result that would falsify it. The wait is set by the audit: D1 at +2 days, D2–7 at +8, D7 at +8, D30 at +31. Anything judged sooner is being judged on an immature cohort.

9

Today's five updates

start immediatelytime-critical

This runs first in wall-clock time, whatever the research schedule says. Monkey, Seal, Brainrot, Verity and Waterslide are shipping today; a pre-update baseline that is not frozen before exposure spreads cannot be reconstructed afterwards. Success and failure thresholds are written down before any post-update number is read, in baselines/, which already exists for exactly this.

Held separately from the historical research, and read with interrupted-time-series and placebo cut dates rather than before/after — the naive test on Seal's last update returned −71% DAU at p = 0.002 and was wrong, because the same test fires on days when nothing shipped.

K

What we do not have

Home impressions before 2026-05-01

The RFY series simply do not exist earlier. For Plane Training that is 457 of 542 game-days where we can see what happened but never what Roblox saw.

Technical history before ~July 2026

Aged out permanently. 8–551 blind days per game. Absence must never be read as health.

Error message text

Volumes by MessageHash exist on all 12; the hash→text lookup is async and the grabber does not poll it, so signatures have counts and no words.

Competition and Discovery-side changes

Roblox names these as reach factors alongside game changes. We observe none of them, which caps every common-shock conclusion at "consistent with".

Funnels on the long-history games

Poison Treats, Knife Farm, Climb Up Mountain and Don't Wake Police have none — a dev-side instrumentation ask, not a grab change.

Country-level ARPDAU

Country splits exist for reach and DAU but the revenue-by-country cut is not in the plan, so the market-mix explanation for ARPPU gaps stays untested.
