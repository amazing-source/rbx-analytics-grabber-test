Four constraints that reshape the brief

1
Reconstruct each lifecycle
fully supported
Objective pullback rule fixed
before
looking at any other metric: J0 is the first day at or
after the all-time Home-reach peak where the 3-day mean falls below a threshold of that peak and stays
below for 3 consecutive days. Sensitivity-tested at 0.60 / 0.70 / 0.80 and at 3 / 5 / 7 hold days; a
result that only survives one setting is reported as such.
Reach is
UniqueUsersWithImpressions
filtered to
HomeRecommendation
— not the all-source total, which mixes in Friends and Search
and blunts the very signal being dated.
2
Leading indicators of pullback
supported
retention cannot lead
Event-study on the J−28…J+28 windows as briefed. Two disciplines carried over from the work that has
already failed on this question: every direction test gives
both
arrows the same lag search and
a multiplicity correction — an asymmetric search manufactured five false "signal leads reach" results on
Waterslide last week — and every candidate break reports its
smear fraction
, the share of candidate
dates that also fire, because a trending series accepts a step almost anywhere.
Per the maturation table, D7 and D30 are structurally incapable of leading and enter as outcomes only.
3
Explore → expand → degradation
supported
The discriminating test, and the one already run on two games: partial correlation of each signal
against log Home reach, controlling for a linear trend
and
weekday. The trend control is not
optional — without it, reach quintiles on a young game are almost perfectly collinear with
time-since-launch (r = +0.81 on Seal), and novelty decay reads as audience dilution.
Dilution is PTR falling while bounce, playtime and play-days hold. Failure at scale is PTR falling
with
bounce rising and play-days falling. Those get different reports.
4
Acquisition-source quality
fully supported, all 12
Conversion, D7, 7-day playtime and payer quality per source, weighted by
UniqueUsersWithPlaySessions
— the denominator. Rates alone mislead: on Seal,
Friends converts at 40.5% against Home's 19.5%, which reads as the better channel until you see 343,887
users against 2,084,897.
Kept strictly separate from Phase 2: aggregate acquisition quality is not an RFY ranking signal, and
Roblox states the ranking runs on RFY-acquired behaviour specifically.
5
Funnels
4 games
2 thin
2 unusable
Reconstruct the join → first action → first reward → progression → second-loop sequence where the
steps exist, split by device, platform, acquisition source and place version.
A step's level is not a finding.
A step sitting at 40% completion means nothing without a
baseline it moved from — and on most of these games there is no baseline to move from. Funnels
localise
a churn detected elsewhere; they do not lead the diagnosis. Where a step does collapse,
the chain is metric → feedback text → QA repro → Studio code, and the output is
"phone players fail tutorial step 4 after build X", not "bounce got worse".
6
Common shock vs independent failure
supported on reach
weak on RFY
Both alignments — calendar date, and days relative to each game's own peak — on weekday-and-trend
residualised log reach. If several games lose residual reach on the same calendar dates while their own
signals hold, platform or seasonality gains credibility. If declines line up better against each game's
own lifecycle and are each preceded by that game's own deterioration, independent causes win.
The pre/post-June arm runs on reach and behaviour, not on RFY, for the reason in constraint 01.
7
Prediction
hypothesis generation only
Leave-one-game-out, time-ordered, features frozen at J−1, logistic regression before anything with
more capacity, compared against a "days since peak" baseline that will probably be hard to beat. Reported
with false positives and negatives by name, and sensitivity to the pullback definition.
With ~12 events this cannot establish that we have recovered anything about Roblox's ranking. If the
model beats the baseline it is a hypothesis worth a prospective test on the next pullback, and that is
the strongest claim available.
8
Intervention mapping
supported
Each recurring pattern gets an action, the metric that should move if we are right, the maturation-aware
wait before judging it, and the result that would falsify it. The wait is set by the audit: D1 at +2 days,
D2–7 at +8, D7 at +8, D30 at +31. Anything judged sooner is being judged on an immature cohort.
9
Today's five updates
start immediately
time-critical
This runs first in wall-clock time, whatever the research schedule says.
Monkey, Seal, Brainrot,
Verity and Waterslide are shipping today; a pre-update baseline that is not frozen before exposure spreads
cannot be reconstructed afterwards. Success and failure thresholds are written down before any post-update
number is read, in

Held separately from the historical research, and read with interrupted-time-series and placebo cut

was wrong, because the same test fires on days when nothing shipped.

K
What we do not have
Home impressions before 2026-05-01
The RFY series simply do not exist earlier. For Plane Training that is 457 of 542 game-days where we can see what happened but never what Roblox saw.
Technical history before ~July 2026
Aged out permanently. 8–551 blind days per game. Absence must never be read as health.
Error message text
Volumes by
MessageHash
exist on all 12; the hash→text lookup is async and the grabber does not poll it, so signatures have counts and no words.
Competition and Discovery-side changes
Roblox names these as reach factors alongside game changes. We observe none of them, which caps every common-shock conclusion at "consistent with".
Funnels on the long-history games
Poison Treats, Knife Farm, Climb Up Mountain and Don't Wake Police have none — a dev-side instrumentation ask, not a grab change.
Country-level ARPDAU
Country splits exist for reach and DAU but the revenue-by-country cut is not in the plan, so the market-mix explanation for ARPPU gaps stays untested.
