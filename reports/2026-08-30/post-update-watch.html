Post-update check/ 4 universes/ Grabbed 2026-08-30 19:21 UTC

Four Publishes, One Day

All four games shipped on 28 August, within six hours of each other. Two days later there is exactly one settled day of post-publish data — and it is a Saturday.

Verdict

No publish broke anything measurable. Nothing on 08-29 moves beyond its own weekday baseline on any game that can be scored. The findings worth acting on are all pre-existing — they were true before these updates and remain true after.

Verity and Seal have their own causes running underneath this window, and those are the ones that decide the next month. They are diagnosed separately in Eight Days Behind.

03The weekend confound

Why "everything went up on 08-29" says nothing on its own.

28 August was a Friday; 29 August a Saturday. Friday → Saturday is the strongest natural uplift in the Roblox week, so every game rising on 08-29 is the expected shape whether or not anything shipped.

Scoring each day against what that weekday normally does for that game removes it. On 08-29, nothing exceeds 2 sd on either game with enough history to score:

The publish did not break it Steady

Seal is the only game with genuine hourly data spanning its own publish. Across v908 at 21:36 UTC, client crash rate stays flat at 0.0035–0.0066 and average FPS holds 57.8–64.6 with no step at the boundary. OOM exits sit at 46 per 100k DAU on 08-29 against a 38–53 band all month.

The DAU drop is a spike unwinding, not a regression Watch

DAU fell 533,776 → 417,306 across the publish, but 08-28 was itself an outlier: 08-27 was 356,255, so 08-29 sits above the pre-spike level. Engagement improved on the same day — play time per DAU 17.61 → 19.56 min, session length 13.90 → 15.01 min, ARPDAU 1.33 → 1.94.

Today is tracking ahead: at 18:00 UTC concurrents read 7,065 against 6,346 at the same hour yesterday. The returning cohort also grew +24% on 08-29 while new users fell 31% — so the dip is acquisition, not players leaving.

The acquisition side is the one to watch. Seal's D1 retention reached 11.2% on 08-28 and its home-recommendation traffic fell 29% on 08-29. That is the same sequence that preceded Verity's traffic being cut — see the companion diagnosis.

Client and server variants together are 5.3M events, 70% of everything this game emits. But the mechanism is a sound that does not play — by the reporting rule that is cosmetic, and it is a textbook case of the loudest signature in an export being the cheapest one. It matters here mainly because it is what is distorting the error metric.

Worth fixing anyway: the asset is failing moderation approval, which is a one-line fix and would restore the error series to a usable signal.

PlayerProgression: FirstMorph 100% → OnboardingComplete 100% → FirstUpgrade 99.3% → Rebirth1 38.25% → Rebirth2 70.6% → Rebirth3 88.6% → Rebirth4 91.55%.

Perf and crash history age out at roughly 30 days and error history at 56 — anything not captured before late September is gone permanently.

Two things that change how a fix should ship

Prefer a config change to a build. The Open Cloud Configs API changes live balance without publishing — propagation is about five minutes, it carries full version history, and it can be rolled back if a metric moves the wrong way. A republish costs a deploy plus a fresh set of PlaceVersion steps to disentangle from every metric afterwards, which is precisely the noise this report had to work around. For anything that is a number rather than a behaviour, use the config.

Expect the deploy's own signature and do not read it as breakage. Session duration and FPS dip in the publish hour and recover within about two hours as servers cycle; error counts can spike 20–50× on the publish day alone. A grab taken the day after a deploy will show all of it. Degradation still present three hours later is a finding; inside the publish hour it is the deploy.

What to watch instead of DAU

Since 15 June, Roblox ranks on play days per user, playtime per user, play-through rate and first-play bounce — over a 28-day window, counting only organically-discovered players. QPTR was removed from ranking entirely, so listing and thumbnail work no longer buys distribution.

The number to track on all four games is RFYL7PlayDays. It reads 1.28 on Verity and 1.32 on Seal — roughly one or two sessions per player per week, which is the ceiling every reach gain gets multiplied by. The causes and the fixes are in Eight Days Behind.
