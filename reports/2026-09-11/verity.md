## Interpretation

*Separated deliberately. The sections above are measurements; this one is not.*

**Confirmed — this is not a reach cut.** Impressions moved −4.4% on a matched
window while conversion fell −25.8% and DAU −23.4%. Roblox kept showing the game
to roughly the same number of people; fewer of them chose to play. That is the
opposite of Seal and Monkey, and it points at the listing rather than at
distribution.

**Likely — an appeal problem, and bounce agrees.** Play-through −33.4% is the
steepest in the pack, and `RFYDuration0To60` rose +10.7% — of those who did join,
more left inside a minute. Those two move together in an appeal story: the
listing is attracting fewer, and converting worse, the ones it does attract.

**Confirmed and going the other way — monetization improved materially.** ARPU
+24.7%, paying-user CVR +15.5%, and **revenue +0.3% on DAU −23.4%**. Because
paying CVR moved, this is more payers per player, not just bigger spenders. This
is the strongest monetization reading of the five games.

**Unresolved — whether the 09-04 lift was the update or its event.** The `NEW
UPDATE` event annotation starts 09-04 19:00, 40 minutes before v684 goes live at
19:40. Event-driven attention and a content ship are confounded here and I cannot
separate them with two days of data on either side.

**Not assessed — v689 (daily + group rewards).** Two days of data, one of them
the publish day. Daily-reward mechanics act on the D1→D2 hold specifically, which
needs roughly a week. **The first clean read is around 09-15.**

**Ruled out:** a discovery/reach explanation (impressions flat); weekday shape
(matched windows).

## Addendum 2026-09-11 (b) — what in v684 plausibly did it

*Interpretation, not measurement.*

v684's headline feature is a **paid personality/skin shop** — each skin changes
messaging style, appearance and message-box colour, the first is free after 50
messages, and further personalities sell at 99R. The measured monetization move:

| | before | after | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARPU | R$0.4763 | R$0.5938 | **+24.7%** |
| Paying-user CVR | 0.82% | 0.95% | **+15.5%** |
| Revenue | R$215,982 | R$216,710 | **+0.3% on DAU −23.4%** |

**Paying CVR moving is the important part** — it means more players *became*
payers, not that existing payers spent more. A newly-introduced paid cosmetic
tier with a free first unlock is a mechanism that does precisely that: the free
skin demonstrates the category, the 99R ones convert. This is a clean and
plausible link, and it is the best monetization result in the pack.

**What it does not explain:** the conversion collapse at the top of the funnel
(impressions flat, impression→play CVR −25.8%, play-through −33.4%, new users
−30.9%). Nothing in v684 touches the store listing, and the alternative-endings
work — the item that might have changed how the game presents — shipped with the
**Best-friends ending DELAYED**. So v684 plausibly improved monetization and did
not address, and probably did not cause, the acquisition problem.

**v689 is where the retention mechanics landed** (daily rewards, returning-player
rewards, like/group reward). Those act on D1→D2 and on lapsed-player return, and
they have had two days. Not assessed — first clean read ~09-15.
