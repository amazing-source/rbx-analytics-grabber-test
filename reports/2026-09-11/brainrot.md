## Where this sits on the lifecycle

Brainrot peaked **2026-07-25 at 776,393 DAU** and has been decaying since. It
now runs at **10–12% of peak** and, uniquely in this portfolio, **has stopped
falling** — 9% of peak on 09-01, 12% on 09-10. Every other game in the pack is
still descending toward that level.

This matters for every number above: the game is deep post-peak, so a flat
volume reading is not stagnation, it is the decay arresting.

## Interpretation

*Separated deliberately. The sections above are measurements; this one is not.*

**Likely — the engagement gain is real and global to the game.** D1 rose on
**every** acquisition source: HomeRecommendation +29.4%, Others +20.6%, Search
+9.7%, **Friends +5.6%**. Friends traffic arrives by social invite and never sees
a thumbnail or the icon test; Search went looking for the game by name. A traffic
-mix or creative explanation predicts those two stay flat. They did not, so the
change is in the game, not in who showed up.

**Likely — the order is product-first, reach-second.** Session length, D1
playtime and qualified play sessions moved on 09-03/09-04; impressions and DAU
followed on 09-04/09-06. Roblox states an impression change typically *follows* a
move in play-through, play days or playtime. That is the sequence here, and it is
the healthy direction of it.

**Unresolved — v608 cannot be given the credit cleanly.** D1, play-through and
bounce all departed on **09-03**, before the 09-04 12:17 publish. Either the
improvement began before the build, or the effect is being read one day early. I
cannot separate those two without knowing what v608 contained.

**Confirmed and pulling the other way — monetization fell.** ARPU −12.2% and
revenue −10.3% on a matched window where DAU was only −4.4%, so this is not
audience size. Paying-user CVR was flat at +3.3%, which locates the change in
**spend per payer, not in how many people pay**.

**Ruled out:** composition (D1 up on all four sources); a partial-day artifact
(every day quoted is settled); weekday shape (both windows are Sat·Sun·Mon·Tue·Wed).

## What this does not establish

- **No causal claim for v608.** Its content is unknown to me and the first
  movement precedes it by a day.
- **The icon swaps of 09-05 are uncontrolled.** Eleven-plus icon changes inside
  the window; I have not event-studied them against the base rate of ordinary
  days, and per the method that test is required before crediting creative.
- **Why ARPU fell is not diagnosed.** Revenue by `RevenueSource` would split
  passes from dev products and is the next query.
- `RFYD2To7PlayDays` fell **−2.8%** here and on all five games. A signal that
  moves the same way on five unrelated universes is a candidate for a platform
  or telemetry effect, not five independent findings.

## What would change this

A revenue-source split showing the ARPU fall is one product. The v608 changelog.
An event-study of the 09-05 icon swaps against non-swap days.

## Addendum 2026-09-11 (b) — what in v608 plausibly did it

*Interpretation, not measurement.*

v608's item 5 is a **quest track available only to new players**, with a **4-day
timer** and a day-3 reward labelled as new-player-exclusive. The measured picture
is:

| | |
|---|---|
| New-user DAU | **+7.1%** (returning −6.6%) |
| D1 retention | **+19.7%**, up on all four acquisition sources and all four real platforms |
| RFY D1 qualified sessions | +8.4% |
| RFY **D8–28** play days | **+42.7%** |

A time-boxed reward ladder aimed exclusively at new players is a mechanism that
would produce exactly this shape — more new players staying past day 1, and a
multi-day track pulling the longer-horizon play-days signal up behind it. **Of
the five updates in this window, this is the closest feature-to-metric match.**

**Two things stop it being a finding:**

1. **Timing.** D1, play-through and bounce first departed on **09-03**, and v608
   shipped **09-04 12:17**. A day-early start is not explained by a build that
   was not yet live. Either something else began the move and v608 extended it,
   or the departure date is a day soft.
2. **No control.** No A/B ran, and a 4-day timer means the cohort exposed to it
   is still inside the measurement window. The honest test is whether the gain
   decays when the first cohorts' timers expire — readable from about **09-12**.

**Also in v608 and not separable:** new Exotic-rarity limited brainrot, new morphs
and a reworked lucky block. Those are collection/monetization surfaces, and
monetization went **down** (ARPU −12.2%), so they are not carrying the engagement
gain — but I cannot rule out that one of them depressed ARPU while the quest
lifted retention. Revenue by `RevenueSource` would separate them.
