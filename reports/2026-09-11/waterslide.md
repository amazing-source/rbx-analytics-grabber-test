09-07 17:00. A `NEW WORLD` event **ended** at the same minute, 09-03 18:00 — one

started. This game is multi-place and the deploy log is per-place — a known
failure mode (F12) — so treat the root row as "no evidence of a root publish",
not as proof there was none.

**Update contents** (spec filed at [`data/updates/10030829154-climb-waterslide-and-slide.md`](../../data/updates/10030829154-climb-waterslide-and-slide.md)):
scripted admin abuse, performance/QoL bugs, **Raft Ascension** (a 200-Win roulette
for five cosmetic raft mutations — no gameplay bonus, explicitly a **Win sink**),
new pet icons, new main-screen offers in the last two worlds, and a **new limited
"Autumn Harvest Egg"** at the same price and chances.

## Headline

Waterslide tracked **close to its own decline** — the update neither rescued nor
worsened it much. The in-game experience is flat; the loss is at conversion. One
funnel step regressed, and everything downstream of it carries the loss.

## Interpretation

*Separated deliberately. The sections above are measurements; this one is not.*

**Likely — a listing/conversion problem, not an in-game one.** Session length
(−0.4%), play minutes per DAU (−0.4%) and RFY D1 playtime (−0.1%) are all flat,
and bounce actually *improved* (−5.2%). Players who arrive behave exactly as
before. What fell is getting them in: CVR −20.9% and play-through −17.1%, against
impressions −18.1%.

**Notable — `Friends` D1 is dead flat at −0.4% while `Search` fell −25.3%.**
Friends never sees a thumbnail; Search came looking by name. The creative-led
reading predicts the opposite ordering, so this does not look like a thumbnail
problem either. A −25% fall confined largely to Search is unusual and I do not
have an explanation for it.

**Likely — the funnel regression is real but small relative to the decline.** A
2pp loss at step 5 compounds through ten downstream steps, but it cannot produce
a −34% DAU move. It is worth fixing on its own merits; it is not the story.

**Ruled out:** an in-game experience regression (every engagement rate flat);
weekday shape (matched windows); the `ADMIN ABUSE` hour as a driver (one hour
inside a five-day mean).

## What this does not establish

- **What the update contained**, or whether a root-place publish happened at all.
- **Why Search D1 fell 25%** while every other source moved <7%.
- The 09-05 `ADMIN ABUSE` event inflates that day's volumes; it is in both the
  daily table and the "after" mean, though its weight is small.

## What would change this

The update's contents. A read of the step-5 code path — per the method, a funnel
step materially below its neighbours is a defect candidate *even with a clean
error log*, and this one has been below its neighbours for weeks.

## Addendum 2026-09-11 (b) — the update's shape matches "barely moved"

*Interpretation, not measurement.*

The headline feature, **Raft Ascension**, is explicitly a **Win sink**: a 200-Win
roulette for five *cosmetic* raft mutations with, in the spec's own words, "no
gameplay bonuses". A cosmetic currency sink is not a mechanism that acquires
players or improves a first session, and the measurements agree — in-game rates
flat, conversion and reach down, D1 mildly down.

The **"Autumn Harvest Egg"** ships at the same price and same chances as existing
eggs, so it is content rather than a monetization change; ARPU fell 17.2%.

**Nothing in this update targets the funnel step that is actually leaking.**
`Tutorial_Main_5_ToRaftShop` lost another 2pp and carries the loss through ten
downstream steps, and it has been the worst step in this funnel for weeks. The
21.08 spec did contain a tutorial bug fix described as "causes some significant
churn" — so the step is known to the studio — but the 03.09 update does not
touch it.
