## Where this sits on the lifecycle

Monkey peaked **2026-08-05 at 975,727 DAU**. It now runs at **5% of peak** — the
lowest of the five, and **below the ~9–12% floor** at which Brainrot's decay
arrested. Its pre-publish trend was already −8.0%/day on DAU.

So the decline predates the update by a month. The question this document answers
is not "why is it declining" but **"did anything change on top of the decline"**.

## Interpretation

*Separated deliberately. The sections above are measurements; this one is not.*

**Likely — this is a product change, not a discovery cut, and the ordering is the
evidence.** Session length, conversion and playtime per DAU all broke on the
publish day; impressions did not move until two days afterwards. Roblox states an
impression decline typically *follows* a fall in play-through rate, play days or
playtime, and that is the order observed. Reading the reach collapse as the cause
inverts it.

**Likely — the cause is global to the game, and creative is ruled out.** D1 fell
on every acquisition source, **including `Friends` (−5.3%)**, which arrives by
social invite and never sees a thumbnail, and `Search` (−12.3%), which came
looking for the game by name. Per the method, when `Friends` falls the creative
cannot be the explanation. `HomeRecommendation` fell hardest in effect size
(−24.3%), which also rules out the opposite story — that a traffic expansion
diluted the average.

**Likely — a first-session problem specifically.** `RFYDuration0To60` rose
**+21.7%**: more players leaving inside the first minute. It is the only metric
in the pack that moved in the "worse" direction by that margin, and it pairs with
session length −10.3% and D1 −19.9%. Something in the first minute of the
experience got worse around 09-04.

**Unresolved — whether v1246/auras caused it.** The timing is exact (session
length departs on the publish day) and the direction is uniform. A publish-day dip
in session length is also the ordinary signature of servers cycling, but that
recovers within ~2 hours and this has not recovered in five days.
**See Addendum (c): the v1246-filtered error export has since been run and found
nothing that scales — 0.11% of error volume. Read that before acting on this
section.**

**Ruled out:** composition and creative (Friends and Search both fell); weekday
shape (matched windows); partial days (all settled).

## What this does not establish

- **No named defect.** This document says a regression is likely and where to
  look; it does not identify code. The funnel here has only three instrumented
  steps (`Joined` → `Punched` → `Collected a Banana`), and the two real ones fell
  only −1.5 and −1.6pp, which is too coarse to localise a first-minute problem.
- **No error-signature claim.** Not examined for this document.
- **The `x5 BONUS EVENT` on 09-06** sits inside the "after" window. It was one
  hour, so its weight on a five-day mean is small, but it is not zero.

## What would change this

The v1246 changelog. A `--version v1246` error export filtered to the build
(`npm run export:errors -- 10399712766 --version 1246 --window 7d`), which this
game can now self-serve. Deeper funnel instrumentation — three steps cannot
locate a first-minute break.

## Addendum 2026-09-11 (b) — reading v1246 against what moved

*Interpretation, not measurement.*

v1246 is a **new map** (visuals only) plus the **Aura system** — a collection and
monetization layer: six rarities of aura carrying small boosts, sold in cash and
Robux crates from a separate stall, duplicates converting to dust for more rolls.

**It did not monetize.** Paying-user CVR **−24.9%** and ARPU −6.3% on the matched
window, against a game whose ARPU had been *rising* through the decline as
lower-value players left. A new crate economy that fails to lift payer conversion
in its first week is a result worth having on its own.

**Nothing in v1246 obviously explains a first-minute regression** — and that is
what the data shows (bounce <60s +21.7%, session −10.3%, D1 −19.9%, worst on
Computer and Console). A new map in the rotation is the only item that touches
the early experience, and a visuals-only map is a weak candidate.

**So the honest position is unchanged from the main document:** the timing is
exact and the direction uniform, but the feature list does not contain an obvious
mechanism, and I have not read the code. The next step is the error export
filtered to v1246 — this game can self-serve it now — and specifically whether
anything in the new map or the aura stall throws on join.

## Addendum 2026-09-11 (c) — the v1246 error export, run

`npm run export:errors -- 10399712766 --version 1246 --window 7d`, and the
unfiltered 7-day export alongside it for scale. Both filed under
`data/exports/10399712766-banana-monkey-destruction/errors/`.

**v1246 introduced essentially no error volume.**

| | events, 7 days |
|---|---|
| All signatures, all versions | **600,812** |
| First seen in **v1246** | **659** — **0.11%** |

The version split of the unfiltered export puts the volume on builds that predate
the update by weeks: V1228 309,671 · V1207 151,509 · V1197 130,814 · V1246 659.

**The one v1246 signature with a plausible mechanism, and it is too small:**

    ReplicatedStorage.Client.Modules.UI.CratesController:856:
    attempt to index nil with 'CameraSubject'          — 277 events, Error/Client

`CratesController` is the aura crate UI this very update shipped, and
`CameraSubject` is nil before a character exists, so this is the "check, yield,
use" shape — it will fire when the crate UI opens early in a session. That makes
it first-session-adjacent, which is the right shape. **But 277 events over seven
days on a game serving ~70,000 DAU cannot move D1 by 19.9%.** It is a real bug
and it is not this one.

**Errors per player went DOWN, not up.** The unfiltered 1-day export of 09-08
carried 314,346 events; the 7-day export today carries 600,812, i.e. ~85,800/day
against ~314,000/day, while DAU fell ~53% over the same stretch. The
`AnimationTrack limit of 64 tracks exceeded` signature went 57,174/day → ~20,771/day.
Whatever happened, it did not make the game throw more per player.

### This is the fourth converging negative

| Instrument | Verdict on a v1246 defect |
|---|---|
| D1 by `AcquisitionSource` (`Friends` fell) | creative and traffic-mix **ruled out** |
| D1 by `Platform` (Computer/Console fell hardest) | render/performance regression **ruled out** |
| Funnel | 3 steps only — too coarse to localise, no break visible |
| **Error export, v1246-filtered** | **0.11% of volume; nothing that scales** |

**So the position has to change.** The main document says "the timing is exact and
the direction uniform" and calls a regression *likely*. The timing and the
ordering still stand — engagement moved on the publish day, reach followed two
days later, and that is product-side rather than discovery-side. **But no
instrument identifies a defect, and the error export was the last one that could
have.**

Per this repo's own rule, **converging negatives are a result.** The last time
this exact game produced this exact pattern, the conclusion "Banana Monkey is the
only game of five with a genuine product regression" was retracted after Ren
played it and found nothing — twice. I am not going to repeat that by escalating.

**Revised reading:** something about v1246 reduced session length and D1 from the
publish day, it is not visible to any error, performance, creative or mix test,
and the remaining candidates are **non-throwing content changes** — the new map
in the rotation, or the aura crate flow pulling players into menus early. Both
are design questions, answerable by playing it, not by another query.

### Two standing defects worth a line, neither caused by v1246

- **`AnimationTrack limit of 64 tracks exceeded, new animations will not be played`**
  — ~20,771/day. On a punching game, animations silently not playing is core-verb
  degradation. Pre-existing (V1228 band), tracks DAU, not a v1246 regression.
- **`EditableImage is not accessible. Go to the Security Tab in Experience
  Settings`** — 2,472. Names its own one-toggle fix, same as Seal had.
