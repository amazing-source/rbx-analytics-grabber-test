Settled today

At the two-day read we could not credit v908 for the revenue jump, because publish day was 87% pre-update. 29 August is the first clean day and it confirms it — DevProduct share went 8.7% to 25.4% on the first full day of the new build and held there. The result is real and it belongs to this update.

The problem: players are leaving before they seal anything

Three separate measurements say the same thing, and none of them depends on the others.

1. A third of new players never reach the driveway

Once a player actually starts sealing, they finish — 93% and 95% on the middle steps. The loss is almost entirely at one step: getting from spawn to a driveway to work on. That is the single biggest lever on this game, and it has fallen every day since 20 August.

2. Four in ten quit within three minutes

Two of the Roblox ranking signals measure exactly this. RFYDuration0To60 — players who leave inside a minute — went 11.87% to 14.58% across the week. RFYDuration61To180 — the next two minutes — went 21.04% to 25.03%. Together that is 39.6% of first plays ending inside three minutes, up from 32.9%. Meanwhile RFYPlayThroughRate fell 31% and RFYD1Playtime fell 16%, while the return signals stayed flat — the exact inverse of Verity.

3. Sentiment has collapsed

Net rating per 100,000 players ran 70–130 for most of August. It is now 6.5 — a fall of roughly 90%, meaning downvotes have almost caught up with upvotes. Lifetime the game sits at 43% downvotes. This turned at the same time as the funnel step and the bounce rate, which is what makes the three readings one finding rather than three.

Worth being clear about

None of this is v908’s doing. All three measures turned on 25–26 August, two to three days before the update shipped. The monetization change is clean; it landed on a game whose front door was already deteriorating.

We already know what is wrong with the first three minutes

This is no longer a question waiting on player feedback. The defect report read against v908 traced ten faults to source, nine of them confirmed, and three of those sit directly on the first session. Two further problems are known from playing the build and are not in that report.

Confirmed Desktop has no performance preset DrivewayClient:179 — touch devices get a coarser grid and roughly 4× less work per frame. Desktop always runs maximum settings with no rung below it. Measured: Script_DrivewayClient is 72–85% of the whole foreground thread, and the engine classified 100% of frames as CPU-bound. A weak PC is unplayable while a phone is fine.
Confirmed The squeegee sweep has no frame budget SurfaceGrid:1596 — the blade pass is deliberately exempt from the frame budget, so it is the one path that can run long: 0.9 ms on a bare slab, 12 ms on a flooded one, and 2.8× worse when the player sweeps diagonally. This is the “it only lags when you use the squeegee” report, and it reproduces on a stock blade at level one.
New The tutorial UI is too large on mobile Not in the defect report — found in play. On a phone the tutorial panel takes enough of the screen that the thing it is pointing at is hard to see or reach. Mobile is the majority platform, and this sits on the exact step the funnel says players fail.
New The tutorial takes about three minutes to reach the core verb Also found in play. This is the finding the data was pointing at all along: 39.6% of first plays end inside three minutes, and the tutorial does not get a player to sealing a driveway inside that window. The two numbers describe the same event from opposite sides.
Confirmed Sealing silently disabled by a place setting DrivewaySurface:56 logs [DrivewaySealer] Sealing is disabled: this place cannot use the Editable Image/Mesh APIs — the warning names its own fix. A minority path, but it sits on the core verb, and it costs one checkbox.

What we want to do about it

Priority 1

Cut the tutorial to the core verb, and fix its mobile layout

target: ReachedDriveway drop-off 34% → under 20%

Get a player pouring sealer on a driveway inside the first minute, not the third. Everything else the tutorial teaches — trucks, upgrades, districts, rebirths — can be taught after the player has done the thing the game is named after. On mobile the panel has to shrink enough that the target it points at stays visible and reachable.

This is the only step in the funnel that loses meaningful numbers, it is the step players complain about, and it sits in front of the packs that are now selling well. Roughly 390,000 players a day enter this funnel and about 134,000 of them never reach a driveway — every one recovered is a player the new monetization can reach. We are deliberately not putting a Robux figure on that: we cannot yet measure what a recovered player is worth against one who was always going to bounce.

Priority 2

Give desktop a quality preset, and put a frame budget on the blade

DrivewayClient:179 · SurfaceGrid:1596 · both confirmed in the defect report

These two are the measured reason the first minutes feel bad on a weak machine, and they compound the tutorial problem — a player who is lagging during a three-minute tutorial is the most likely person in the game to leave inside three minutes.

Both fixes are written out in the defect report, with the caveat that the preset must be applied at join — CellSize is baked into every grid at construction — and that the blade should be strided, not frame-skipped, so the tool keeps the responsiveness the exemption exists to protect.

Priority 3 · free, one setting

Turn on Mesh & Image APIs

Game Settings → Security → Allow Mesh & Image APIs

One checkbox, and the warning names it exactly. Note that it needs a republish to take effect, which puts it in the queue below — it is the first thing that will want to jump it.

A decision, not a task

Hold the next publish until 5 September if it can wait

protects the only read of the D3/D7 reward rework

The reward rework that shipped in v908 can only be judged on 5 September, when ForwardD7Retention matures for its cohort. A new PlaceVersion before then sits across that measurement and we lose the answer permanently.

The tutorial work will not be ready before the 5th anyway, so in practice this costs us only the Mesh & Image toggle. Our recommendation: hold it. The sealing warning is a minority path and the reward read is a one-time opportunity. If something genuinely urgent must ship, ship it — but do that knowingly.
